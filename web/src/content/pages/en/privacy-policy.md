## Data collected

The contact form collects only:

- your name;
- your email address;
- the content of your message;
- the date it was sent.

No data is collected without your knowledge. Fields are limited to what is
strictly necessary to reply to you.

## Purpose and legal basis

This data is used **only to reply to your message**. The legal basis is your
consent, given by ticking the dedicated box before sending.

It is never sold, never used for marketing and never shared with commercial
partners.

## Retention period

Messages are kept for **12 months** from receipt, then deleted. You may request
their deletion earlier.

## Recipients

The data is accessible to Mohamed Hosayni and, technically, to the server host
and the email service used to deliver the message. No other recipient has access
to this data.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access your data;
- rectify it;
- erase it;
- object to its processing;
- request restricted processing.

To exercise these rights, write to
[hosayni92@gmail.com](mailto:hosayni92@gmail.com). You will receive a reply
within one month at the latest.

## Cookies and analytics

This site uses **no advertising cookies and no commercial trackers**.

Your light/dark theme choice is stored in your browser’s local storage
(`localStorage`). That information stays on your device and is never
transmitted.

If privacy-friendly analytics are enabled, they set no cookie and cannot
identify you individually.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Messages are sent through a rate-limited form
protected against automated submissions. Server access is restricted by SSH key.
